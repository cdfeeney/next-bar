## Findings

### F1 — `hiphop` is a DEAD TAG with a LIVE quiz path (worst class; fix before beta)
The quiz music question can emit `hiphop`, but **zero** open bars carry it.
A user who picks hip-hop contributes a tag that can never match — pure
Jaccard dilution of their profile (it inflates the union, shrinking every
score). **Proposal (pick one):** (a) tag the genuinely hip-hop-leaning bars
during B5c-2/3 review-based enrichment, or (b) drop `hiphop` from the quiz
option (map that answer to `dance` + `loud`) until (a) happens. (b) is a
2-line quiz edit and honest today.

### F2 — 11 tags are INEXPRESSIBLE via the quiz
`date` (84 bars!), `beer` (76), `pub` (44), `post-work` (38), `live` (29),
`romantic` (24), `wine` (20), `rooftop` (19), `garden` (17), `splurge`
(10), `tourist` (7). New users cannot express these preferences at all —
they only enter matching through lovedTags affinity (requires rating
history) or neighborhood accident. `date`/`romantic` is the glaring one:
a "date spot tonight" intent is a core bar-choice mode and the #4 tag in
the catalog. **Proposal:** add ONE quiz question ("Who are you out with?"
→ date/romantic · post-work crew · locals hang · showtime live/jazz) and
fold `beer`/`pub`/`wine` into the existing drink question as a third
option. Covers 8 of the 11 without lengthening the quiz beyond +1 screen.
`tourist`/`splurge` are fine staying catalog-only (see F4/F5).

### F3 — Near-duplicate pairs dilute Jaccard (merge candidates for B5c)
Co-occurrence says three pairs behave as one concept:
- `cheap`+`dive`: 42 of 45 dive bars are cheap. `dive` already implies
  cheap in this catalog.
- `beer`+`pub`: 32 of 44 pubs. `pub` is a venue-type, `beer` its drink —
  defensible to keep both, but tagging discipline should require pubs to
  carry `beer` only when beer is the point.
- `buzzy`+`loud`: 30 of 34 loud bars are buzzy — `loud` is nearly a
  stronger `buzzy`. Keep both (the quiz distinguishes them) but B5c review-mining
  should validate `loud` specifically, not co-assign.
**Proposal:** no merges NOW (bitmask append-only rule — a merge is a
catalog-wide rewrite). Instead: B5c-3's review-extraction should treat
these pairs as mutually-implying priors and flag bars carrying one
without evidence for the other.

### F4 — `tourist` (7 bars, 2.9%) reads as a NEGATIVE signal wearing a positive suit
Users don't quiz-select "tourist"; the tag's only real value is as an
avoid-signal for `locals`-leaning profiles, which Jaccard cannot express
(absence of overlap ≠ penalty). **Proposal:** leave the 7 taggings, but
B5c should NOT expand it; longer-term consider a `crowd` axis with
negative weighting instead of a positive tag (blueprint-level change,
operator call).

### F5 — Price tags duplicate `priceTier`
`cheap`/`mid`/`pricey`/`splurge` (127 taggings) restate the structured
`priceTier: 1-4` field. Two sources of truth CAN diverge (a `cheap`-tagged
priceTier-3 bar). **Proposal:** B5c-2 should cross-check tag-vs-tier and
flag mismatches; long-term, derive the price tag from priceTier at
catalog-load time and retire the four tags from the hand-authored set
(keeps the mask stable — derivation happens before masking).

### F6 — Music axis is threadbare outside `indie`
`indie` 31, `live` 29, `jazz` 10, `house` 5, `hiphop` 0. The quiz offers
four music answers but the catalog can only honor two of them. **Proposal:**
B5c-3 review mining should prioritize music-genre evidence (reviews
mention DJs/genres constantly); until then consider quiz music options
reflecting actual coverage (indie · jazz/live · "whatever's playing").

### F7 — Healthy core (no action)
`buzzy`, `cocktail`, `locals`, `chill`, `date`, `beer`, `cheap`, `dive`,
`mid`, `old-nyc`, `polished`, `trendy` all sit in the 15-42% band —
discriminative without being universal. The adaptive Jaccard threshold
(0.25→0.10) plus these densities explains why matching currently feels
sane. Nothing in this audit suggests touching `matching.ts` weights.

## Proposed action order (all deferred to operator / later B5c passes)
1. F1(b) quiz fix for `hiphop` (2 lines, honest immediately) — cheapest win.
2. F2 quiz question addition (+1 screen, unlocks `date`/`romantic` et al.).
3. F5 price cross-check inside B5c-2 (tonight's N8 can note mismatches it
   happens to see, full pass later).
4. F3/F6 priors wired into B5c-3 review-extraction prompts.
5. F4 crowd-axis rethink — blueprint-level, operator decision.
