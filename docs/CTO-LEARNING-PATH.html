Mandatory for consequential work

The nine-factor change brief

Before implementation: predict the risk. After verification: report what actually happened. Unknown is not low risk.

01 · Damage

Blast radius

If wrong, which users, systems, services, data, or company commitments can it damage?

02 · Signal

Detection

Which tests, logs, metrics, alerts, or user-visible signals reveal the failure?

03 · Escape

Recovery

Can it be disabled, rolled back, repaired, restored, or forward-fixed?

04 · Trust

Data

Could users lose data, receive incorrect data, or expose private information?

05 · Power

Access

Which people, services, and credentials can perform or affect this operation?

06 · Exposure

Cost

Can abuse, retries, loops, traffic, or vendor behavior create material expense?

07 · Time

Compatibility

Do older browser sessions and installed iPhone versions still work with the new backend?

08 · Response

Ownership

Who responds outside working hours, and which runbook or escalation path do they use?

09 · Purpose

Business value

Which measurable user or company outcome makes accepting this risk worthwhile?

Every answer should contain a risk rating, plain-language explanation, evidence, mitigation, owner, and unresolved questions. High or critical unresolved risk requires attended approval.
