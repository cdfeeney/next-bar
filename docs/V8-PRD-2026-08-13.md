# Next Bar V8 product requirements

Date: 2026-08-13

Baseline: internal TestFlight `1.0 (7)`

Release target: internal TestFlight only

## Outcome

V8 should feel like a deliberate iPhone app, let a user invite friends into one
real Night Out plan, and deliver useful native invitation notifications without
losing any V7 account data. Work is staging-first. Public App Store submission,
the production database, and the production Vercel alias remain out of scope.

## Proven starting point

- Installing V7 over V6 preserved the operator's signed-in account.
- V7 serves the bar catalog from Supabase and uses compliant live Google Places
  UI Kit media rather than shipping the scrape or cached Google photos.
- Existing code already has shared-night links, friend relationships, nightly
  bar suggestions, RSVPs, branded share routes, and dark web-push scaffolding.
- The iOS app is currently a Capacitor shell loading the staging web origin.
- The native push plugin, iOS push entitlement, APNs device-token flow, and
  notification sender are not present.

## P0: native interaction and design lock

The interaction contract below is locked for V8:

1. Each route has exactly one vertical scroll owner.
2. The app shell never scrolls horizontally and never exposes a browser-style
   horizontal or vertical scrollbar inside the Capacitor WebView.
3. Headers and bottom navigation remain attached to the safe areas. Pulling or
   overscrolling must not reveal a broken gap above the header or move fixed
   navigation with page content.
4. A lightbox or sheet locks the page behind it and owns its own deliberate
   scrolling. Closing it restores the prior scroll position and focus.
5. Horizontal scrolling is allowed only for an intentional carousel. It has no
   visible rail and has an accessible non-swipe path when controls are needed.
6. Filters, chip groups, cards, and bottom navigation wrap or disclose; they do
   not become accidental scroll strips.
7. Every touched screen follows `docs/DESIGN-SYSTEM.md`: one decision, one
   primary action, 44px minimum targets, reduced motion, and no dead ends.
8. Approval is based on a physical Capacitor iPhone build, not desktop Chrome
   resized to phone dimensions.

Acceptance evidence:

- Automated overflow checks at compact and large iPhone viewports for Map,
  Rankings, Next Bar?, Social, Account, and every opened lightbox/sheet.
- Screenshots before and after top/bottom overscroll, route changes, keyboard
  appearance, lightbox open/close, and device rotation.
- Physical iPhone confirmation that no unintended rail or scrollbar appears,
  the header stays visually intact, and the bottom navigation respects its safe
  area.

The V8 art direction is locked by `docs/design-reference/README.md` and
`docs/CLAUDE-DESIGN-RECOVERY-2026-08-12.md`. The canonical navigation is exactly
`Map / Rankings / Next Bar? / Social / Account`, with no sixth tab. The 13
screenshots under `docs/design-reference/approved/` and the recovered visual
tokens are implementation input. `Night editor` and `Route after dark` are audit
hypotheses and must not be built as visual pilots.

The three screenshots under `docs/design-reference/exploratory/` remain review
material, not approved implementation input. V8 may not treat onboarding,
invite-recipient presentation, or operational-state presentation as visually
approved until the founder records that decision. Apple, Google, and phone auth
are outside this V8 queue; email/password remains the implemented auth path.

## P1: Next Bar? ranking contract

Next Bar? displays five recommendations in order. It does not collect a rating,
score, pass, or hide action. `rank` means ordering recommendations; `rate`
means entering a personal score elsewhere in Rankings. No Hide or “never show
me this again” control is part of V8.

The ranker is a cascade, not a global weighted sum:

1. Build the eligible pool from valid/open catalog entries. No rating-based
   suppression applies (see the resolved suppression decision below).
2. Fill five slots from the closest distance band first; expand only when the
   closer band cannot fill five slots.
3. Within the selected band, order by learned taste.
4. Use exact miles only as the final tie-breaker.

Quiz tags are a cold-start prior only. They never permanently contribute a
fixed weight and never gate candidate admission.

The approved learned-evidence model is:

```text
w = (score - 5.5) / 4.5
A(tag) = Σw / (tag observation count + 5)
c = N / (N + 10)
```

Scores are graded evidence: low scores contribute negative evidence, repeated
observations accumulate, and quiz taste dominates only while rating history is
small. The constants are explicit product assumptions and must not be changed
silently. Offline replay/NDCG is verification work, not a user-facing feature.

Implementation records (2026-08-19, operator-approved during the D2 build):

- **Band boundaries reuse the existing distance constants** — `RADIUS_WALK`
  (1.5mi) and `RADIUS_CAB` (4mi), inclusive upper edges. No new distance
  threshold was invented.
- **A bar's learned-taste value is the MEAN of A(tag) over its tags**, not the
  sum. The PRD fixes A(tag) but not how a bar's tags combine; summing would
  reward a bar for merely carrying more tags, which is a catalog artifact
  rather than taste. Recorded here because it is an assumption, not a detail.
- **`LATE_CLUB_BOOST` / `LATE_RESTAURANT_PENALTY` rescaled 0.06 -> 0.12.** The
  2026-07-27 late-night bias was calibrated against the old ranker, where vibe
  entered at `VIBE_WEIGHT = 0.5`. The cascade ranks on the blended taste/prior
  at full weight, halving the nudge's relative authority. The number changed
  so the approved behavior would not.
- **Learned ranker evidence is NOT capped** to the five tags Settings displays;
  `TOP_TAGS_LIMIT` remains a presentation cap in `tasteProfile.ts` only.

Suppression — RESOLVED 2026-08-19 by the founder: **Option B**. A score below
5.0 no longer suppresses a bar. Low scores stop excluding a bar from Next Bar?
and act only as negative evidence in the model above, so a low-scored bar can
still be recommended when it is close and otherwise fits. No control is added
to express this and none is removed. There are now no open product decisions in
this section.

## P0: account and release continuity

- Do not rename, clear, or change the meaning of V7 local-storage keys without a
  tested forward migration.
- Supabase migrations are additive and versioned. Catalog promotion is separate
  from private account data; no user table is reseeded, truncated, or replaced.
- Define one server owner and conflict rule for ratings, numeric scores, named
  lists, vibe profile, night history, shared nights, and notification devices.
- Add a V7-to-V8 upgrade fixture and repeat the physical install-over test using
  Tester 1 / Conor Feeney.
- Required retained state: authentication, Bar 54, named lists, numeric scores
  including ties, night history, and shared-night state.

## P1: real Night Out invitations

User story: a signed-in user creates tonight's plan, invites a friend by account
or link, and both people see the same plan. The recipient can accept or choose
`Not tonight`; accepted members can suggest and vote on bars.

An anonymous bearer link may preview only the explicitly shared plan. Joining,
accepting, declining, suggesting, and voting require authentication; preserving
invite context through that handoff is required. Pre-signup RSVP remains an
exploratory proposal and is not authorized by this PRD.

Required states:

- Draft, open, decided, and cancelled Night Out.
- Pending, accepted, and declined invitation; `Not tonight` is the user-facing
  declined action rather than a second duplicate database state.
- Invite, accepted, bar suggested, and plan changed events.
- A bearer link may preview only explicitly shared plan data. Private ratings,
  scores, account identifiers, and notification tokens never appear in the URL
  or Open Graph payload.

Architecture decision:

- Add one canonical `night_outs` identity and membership/invitation records.
  Keep the existing `shared_nights` route as the public snapshot/link surface.
- Reuse the existing suggestion, RSVP, share-sheet, and branded-unfurl behavior
  where its authorization model fits. Do not create a second generic social
  graph or chat system.
- Scope collaborative suggestions and votes to invited Night Out members. The
  current date-and-follow-circle tables cannot distinguish two separate groups
  planning on the same night and are not sufficient as the canonical object.
- Group chat is excluded from V8; moderation, blocking, retention, abuse, and
  notification-volume policy must exist before chat is considered.

Acceptance evidence:

- Owner creates/cancels; invited account or link recipient accepts/declines;
  nonmember access is denied by RLS/RPC tests.
- Duplicate invite and duplicate event delivery are idempotent.
- Two different groups can plan the same date without data crossing.
- A share link opens the exact plan and a signed-in acceptance returns to it.

## P1: native invitation notifications

V8 notifications are limited to: invited to a Night Out, invitation accepted,
bar suggested, and plan changed. Each notification deep-links to the exact plan.

Architecture decision:

- Use native iOS remote notifications through the Capacitor push-notifications
  plugin and APNs. The existing VAPID/web-push module may remain dark for the
  website, but it is not the TestFlight notification implementation.
- Store device tokens server-side against the authenticated user with platform,
  installation identity, updated timestamp, revocation, RLS, and a per-user cap.
- Generate notification events into a server-owned idempotent outbox. A
  server-side sender delivers to APNs, records success/failure, and removes or
  disables invalid tokens. APNs credentials never enter the client bundle.
- Show an in-app preference first. Request iOS permission only after the user
  sends or accepts an invitation, never on first launch.
- Sign-out and account deletion revoke the current installation token.

Acceptance evidence:

- Real-device foreground, background, and terminated delivery.
- Permission accepted, denied, later revoked, token rotation, duplicate event,
  invalid token, multiple devices, and sign-out cases.
- Notification taps open the intended Night Out after cold launch and after an
  authentication handoff.
- No production sender or credential is enabled during V8 staging work.

## P1: venue presentation and expansion

- Show up to five deterministic customer-facing tags in the bar lightbox, such
  as cocktail, pub, rooftop, nightclub, wine, food, sports, gay bar, lounge, and
  reservation-led.
- Preserve Manhattan as the stabilized core. Hoboken and other approved suburbs
  are a separate catalog lane and enter staging only after the V8 core passes.
- Every added venue requires stable Place ID, operational status, allowed type,
  matching identity/location, and duplicate detection.

## Delivery order

1. Preserve the recovered design package and freeze this reconciled PRD.
2. Apply the locked five-tab visual system while fixing native scroll and
   safe-area behavior; do not redesign exploratory screens.
3. Establish the V7-to-V8 continuity fixture, object ownership, and conflict
   rules before any V8 database-backed feature changes.
4. Implement the canonical Night Out invitation lifecycle.
5. Implement staging-only native APNs registration, outbox, delivery, and deep
   links.
6. Add venue tags, then consider the separately validated expansion catalog.
7. Complete the attended physical V7-to-V8 upgrade tests.
8. Integrate sequentially, deploy to staging, run device suites, and upload a V8
   internal TestFlight build only with attended approval.

## Release gate

V8 is ready for internal TestFlight when the design contract passes on a
physical iPhone, the locked approved visual system is implemented, invitation
and APNs flows pass with staging accounts, V7 account state survives
install-over and force-close/reopen, all migrations are additive, and the full
unit/type/build/browser suites pass with zero retries. TestFlight upload,
database migration, deployment, and any production change remain separate
attended actions.
