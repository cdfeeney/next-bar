### Two things I got wrong in my own first pass — corrected by probe

- **`/rankings` "+ Add a bar" is NOT nav coverage.** Your brief listed it as a
  genuine finding and I repeated that. A direct probe disproves it: at rest the
  control sits at y=648, h=44, so its centre is **cy=670 on a 664px viewport** —
  6px below the fold, where `elementFromPoint` returns `null`. Once scrolled into
  view it is hit correctly by its own element. It was the measurement artifact,
  not a bug. **One fewer real defect than believed.**
- **`window.scrollTo` is a no-op on these routes.** The probe found `maxScroll`
  = 420px while `scrollY` stayed **0** after `scrollTo(0, scrollHeight)` — the app
  scrolls an inner container, not the document. So the *original* spec's
  "scrolled to bottom" pass **never scrolled**; it silently re-measured the top of
  the page and has been dead weight since it was written. Pass 2 now finds and
  drives the real scroller, and immediately caught an at-rest coverage finding on
  Pixel 7 `/` that nothing had seen before.
