## The problem in one paragraph

Next Bar ranks bars with `scoreBar()` in `src/lib/matching.ts`:
`VIBE_WEIGHT·jaccard(userTags, bar.tags) + DIST_WEIGHT·proximity + RATING_WEIGHT·jaccard(bar.tags, lovedTags)`.
Two of three terms are pure functions of `bar.tags`, so **tag quality is the recommendation engine**. Today tags are generated by `heuristicTags()` in `scripts/ingest-bars.ts` — a regex over the bar's *name* plus a small `types` map, defaulting to `['cocktail']` when nothing matches. That cannot tell a dive from a date spot. The job is to replace name-regex tagging with **review-sentiment-derived tagging**, and to mass-import the rest of Manhattan while doing it.

## Scope — settled, do not relitigate

**Product scope is all of Manhattan, not one neighborhood.** Users cross neighborhoods on one night; a neighborhood-scoped bar app breaks at the boundary. Marketing concentration and product coverage are different axes.

Current catalog: **384 bars / 17 neighborhoods**, ~234 Manhattan and ~150 Brooklyn+Queens. **Hell's Kitchen is in the `Neighborhood` type union with zero bars** — a dense bar corridor entirely missing. `scripts/ingest-bars.ts` `AREAS` omits Hell's Kitchen, Harlem, and SoHo while spending 4 of 12 entries on Brooklyn.

Target: **~60 bars per Manhattan neighborhood × 11 ≈ 650**. Outer-borough bars are **not deleted** — they go behind a borough/city scope flag.

## What already exists — extend, don't rewrite

- `scripts/ingest-bars.ts` (343 lines) — discovery. OSM Overpass (free) or Google Places. Writes `scripts/data/candidates.json`, accumulates across runs, dedupes by lowercased name vs `src/lib/bars.ts`. Has a `LOOP_UNATTENDED=1` abort guard.
- `scripts/refresh-places.mjs` (398 lines) — enrichment. Places API (New), FieldMask-scoped. Modes: `--apply`, `--photos`, `--photos-multi`, `--reviews` (monthly, Enterprise field tier), `--only`, `--limit`, `--force-photos`. Writes a **sidecar patch** overlaid at load time so hand-authored bar files stay clean, with a wrong-venue bbox guard that rejects a bad patch wholesale.
- `src/types/index.ts` — `Bar`, `VibeTag` (33 values), `Neighborhood`, `BarReview`, `PlacePatch`.
- `src/lib/constants.ts` — `LAST_VERIFIED_HARD_FILTER_DAYS = 365`, the Jaccard ladder (`JACCARD_START` 0.25 → `JACCARD_FLOOR` 0.10, step 0.05), `MIN_CANDIDATES` 3, `MAX_RESULTS` 3.

**Watch out:** `LAST_VERIFIED_HARD_FILTER_DAYS = 365` hard-filters bars out of results once `lastVerified` is over a year old. Every imported bar must stamp `lastVerified`, and the catalog needs a re-verification story or it silently shrinks on a rolling basis.

## The net-new work: `scripts/infer-vibes.ts`

Reviews + signals → `VibeTag[]` + `priceTier` + `blurb`, via `gemini-2.5-flash`, batched ~20 bars/call.

Output must be strict JSON: `{ tags, priceTier, blurb, confidence }`.

**Non-negotiable constraints:**
- `tags` ⊆ the 33-value `VibeTag` union. **Reject and retry on any out-of-vocab value — never coerce, never silently drop.**
- Exactly one price tag (`cheap`/`mid`/`pricey`/`splurge`), agreeing with `priceTier`.
- Target 4–7 tags. Jaccard punishes both extremes — too few starves the intersection, too many inflate the union denominator.
- `confidence < 0.6` or fewer than 2 reviews → manual review queue, **not** the catalog.
- Add a `LOOP_UNATTENDED=1` abort guard, same as `ingest-bars.ts`. LLM spend in an unattended overnight loop is the same failure mode as Places spend.

**Known limitation, already decided:** Places returns ≤5 reviews and `refresh-places.mjs` truncates to ≤200 chars, so ~1000 chars/bar. Good enough for coarse facets (dive vs cocktail, cheap vs splurge), unreliable for subtle ones (`industry`, `old-nyc`, `romantic`). **Accept this for now.** Do not add a second review source without an explicit operator decision — that is where the ToS risk lives.

## Build order

Follow the table in `docs/PLAN-bar-mass-import.md` §4. Steps 1–2 (AREAS rewrite, borough scope flag) are cheap and unblock everything.

**Step 7 is the real gate: hand-label a 30-bar golden set the operator personally knows, then score model tags against it (Jaccard ≥0.70).** Do this on the 30-bar sample *before* the full ~650-bar spend. Without it there is no way to know whether the sentiment stage improved anything or just swapped one flavor of wrong tag for another.

## Before spending money — ask the operator

1. **Price a 50-bar run first and extrapolate.** Details-with-`reviews` is the Enterprise field tier and materially pricier than basic. Do not assume old pricing. Set a hard ceiling and log actual spend to `scripts/data/import-report.json`.
2. **Decide the review-retention question.** Google's terms restrict how long Places content may be cached and require visible attribution. Safer shape: fetch reviews → derive tags → **persist the derived tags, discard the raw text.** Smaller bundle too. Get an explicit yes before persisting review text to the repo.
