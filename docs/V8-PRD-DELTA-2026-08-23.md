## 2. The 2026-08-23 founder decisions

| # | Change | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Feed is never public.** Its audience is all mutual friends, a named mutual-friend group, or a custom mutual-friend subset. | `D-C-29` |
| 2 | **Feed comments and moderation.** Anyone authorized to view may comment; a commenter deletes their own comment; the post author removes comments from their post; blocking stops visibility and interaction both ways; reporting hides for the reporter immediately and creates a **server-owned** operator-review record; **no new moderation dashboard** in V8; report records persist until operator removal or account deletion; deleting the post removes its comments. | `D-C-30` |
| 3 | **Persistent group chat.** Creator is the initial administrator; administrators rename and add or remove mutual friends; any member may leave; members send text and photos; any member may reuse the current membership to invite the group to a Night Out; senders delete their own messages for everyone; administrators remove messages; messages persist until removed or the group is deleted; blocking prevents new direct interaction, invitations, or addition to new shared groups; reporting as above. **In-app unread state and Night Out invitation notifications — no push per ordinary Group message.** | `D-C-31` |
| 4 | **Captions.** Global composer: optional, **max 140 characters**. Quick Add-to-Story: **caption-free**, deliberately. A Story published through the global composer may carry its caption. | `D-C-32` |
| 5 | **Multi-destination deletion.** One media object, several destination references. *Remove from this destination* removes one; *Delete everywhere* removes all; removing one never destroys the others; **bytes go only when no destination and no Saved Nights Out archive references them**; removing Feed also removes that post's comments. | `D-C-33` |
| 6 | **Success receipts.** Feed only "Posted to Feed" (View post, Undo); Story only "Added to your story" (View story, Undo); multiple "Shared to N places" with indicators (Done, Undo). | `D-C-34` |
| 7 | **Ranking events.** No automatic Feed entries in V8; deferred to V9. | `D-C-35` |
| 8 | **Presence and pin.** No automatic GPS/location tracking; presence is manual; Going out / Maybe later / Not going out; Pin or Heading-to is an explicit action that **sets Going out** and the bar until 4:00 AM; Maybe later or Not going out **clears** the pin; both expire at 4:00 AM; **RSVP never silently controls presence**. Replaces "overrides automatic presence". | `D-C-36` |
| 9 | **Photos library.** Native Photos-library export or save is **entirely deferred to V9**, unconditionally. Saved Nights Out stays in V8 as a private **in-app** archive and is not the Photos library. | `D-C-19` (revised) |

## 3. Group notification volume — the prerequisite the base PRD required

The base PRD made moderation, blocking, retention, abuse **and notification-volume** policy a
precondition of considering group chat. `D-C-31` supplies all of it. The notification-volume half
is recorded explicitly because it was the piece previously omitted:

- V8 provides **in-app unread state** for Group threads.
- V8 sends **Night Out invitation notifications**.
- V8 **does not send a push notification for every ordinary Group message**.
- The `Group activity` notification category defaults to **Mentions only**.

Carried by `V8-R-GRP-008` (policy) and reflected in `V8-R-ACC-010`.

- **Automatic publication of a Story to Feed**, and **duplicated media across destinations** —
  `D-C-14`.

- **Any automatic GPS or location tracking**, and any RSVP-driven change to presence — `D-C-36`.
- **Native Photos-library export** — `D-C-19`.
