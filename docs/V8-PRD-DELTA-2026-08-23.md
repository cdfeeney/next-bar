## 6. Blocked requirements

Not deferred and not missing — specified and approved, but not implementable yet. Two kinds:

**Blocked on founder approval** (`D-P-01` … `D-P-08`) — every Feed-audience, Feed-comment, Group,
caption, multi-destination-deletion, receipt-wording, ranking-event-in-Feed and automatic-presence
row. These stay `blocked` in the ledger; the validator rejects any of them marked approved.

**Blocked on a missing capability**, both confirmed by two reviewer families on candidate
`e0d25ec1`:

| ID | Blocker |
|---|---|
| `V8-R-STO-014` | Server-side re-encode is absent. Uploads go client → Storage directly, so EXIF/GPS stripping is client-side best-effort and bypassable by a modified client — on a surface that promises venue-tagged, never geotagged. |
| `V8-R-STO-015` | Signed-URL lifetime is not server-enforced. `signedUrlTtlSeconds` is a client-side clamp, so an authorized modified client can mint a shareable bearer URL that outlives both expiry and the friend gate. |

Both close only by routing bytes through a server handler that re-encodes (`sharp` is already a
dependency) and mints URLs server-side. `OA-01`/`OA-02` supply the evidence path; they do not
supply the capability.

## 8. Change control

Approval freezes, together and by digest: this delta, the decision record, the ledger, the
authoritative PRD, the design-reference README and all 16 approved canvases. The ledger binds the
first six by SHA-256; the validator emits the ledger's own digest on every run, and that value is
recorded with the approval.

After approval, every goal must pin the release ID, the delta version and digest, its requirement
IDs, exact scope, exclusions, verification and candidate evidence. A later PRD or design change
makes affected goals **stale** until an attended reconciliation explicitly preserves or replaces
their scope. A `complete` goal remains historical evidence; it does not automatically satisfy a
changed requirement.
